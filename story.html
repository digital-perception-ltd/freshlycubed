Our Story

“Freshly Cubed was born from a desire to share with others what helped me.”

Zeba, Founder

After 15 years in consulting and diagnosed with burnout, I started researching ways to boost my immunity. I began making my own cubes because I knew what each ingredient was and its associated properties. Having a cube every morning, quickly became my daily ritual and soon, I was making them for family and friends. When life got busy, I looked for a more convenient option and was surprised to find there wasn’t one. Alas, Freshly Cubed was born.

We’re two former strategy consultants swapping corporate careers for something a little different. Our motivations were personal - burnout, ageing parents and a growing appreciation for the small things we can do to look after ourselves.

Our mission is simple

To make daily wellness effortless, intentional and genuinely good for people and the planet.

We believe looking after yourself isn’t about doing everything perfectly. It’s about small, consistent choices over quick fixes and wellness hype. We’re not here to promise miracles, just to make looking after yourself a little easier.
