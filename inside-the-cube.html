Anatomy of the cube

Five layers of goodness, one cube

Every cube holds the same five organic ingredients. Select a layer to read why it is there.

  1. 01

    Oranges

    Vitamin C

    Naturally contain vitamin C, an essential nutrient that contributes to normal immune function and helps protect cells from oxidative stress

  2. 02

    Lemons

    Vitamin C

    A natural source of vitamin C, which contributes to normal collagen formation for the normal function of skin

  3. 03

    Ginger

    Gingerols

    Contains gingerols, naturally occurring compounds that have attracted scientific interest for their antioxidant and anti-inflammatory activity, as well as their potential role in digestive health

  4. 04

    Turmeric

    Curcuminoids

    Contains curcuminoids, including curcumin, plant compounds widely researched for their antioxidant and anti-inflammatory activity

  5. 05

    Black Pepper

    Piperine

    Contains piperine, a naturally occurring compound studied for its interaction with curcumin and its potential to influence absorption
